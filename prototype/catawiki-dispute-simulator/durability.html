Start the writes. Each record is appended to the leader, copied to its in-sync followers, then acknowledged.

What to remember: A topic partition has one leader replica and follower replicas; these are copies of the same partition, not extra partitions. In-sync replicas are caught up. acks=all waits for acknowledgments from every replica currently in the ISR, including the leader. min.insync.replicas=2 is the minimum ISR size for a write to be accepted. When the leader fails, Kafka elects an in-sync replica; writes may be briefly unavailable during election. This demo assumes replication factor 3, all replicas caught up before failure, and the shown settings; they are teaching values, not Catawiki production configuration.
